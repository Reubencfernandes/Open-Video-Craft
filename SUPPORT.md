# Open Video Craft Support

Open Video Craft is an open-source screen recorder and video editor for macOS
and Windows. Use the options below to ask a question, report a problem, or
request help.

## Ask a question

For setup help, recording and editing questions, or workflow feedback, post in
the [Open Video Craft community discussion](https://github.com/Reubencfernandes/Open-Video-Craft/discussions/7).

## Report a problem

For a reproducible bug, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Reubencfernandes/Open-Video-Craft/issues/new)
and include:

- your Open Video Craft version;
- your macOS or Windows version;
- the steps that caused the problem;
- the error message, if one appeared; and
- a screenshot or short screen recording when useful.

Do not attach private recordings, API keys, or other sensitive project data.

## Common macOS help

- **Screen, camera, or microphone access:** Open System Settings → Privacy &
  Security, grant the requested permission to Open Video Craft, then reopen the
  app.
- **A closed main window:** Choose **Window → Open Main Window** or press
  **Command–0**.
- **Updates:** Direct-download builds update inside the app. Mac App Store
  builds are updated through the App Store.
